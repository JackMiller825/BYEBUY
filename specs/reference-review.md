# Reference review notes

Reviewed official sites and accessible pages on 2026-10-13.

## Observed
- Dogecoin homepage: `https://dogecoin.com/` presents three Getting Started steps (choose wallet, configure wallet, get DOGE), then community and FAQ/resources. Its Dogepedia separates documentation, wallets, usage, community, mining, and development. This makes beginner education easy to find.
- Shiba Inu hub: `https://shib.io/` groups navigation into ecosystem, tokens, community, developers, and contact. This is useful for scanning, although BYEBUY does not need a multi-product ecosystem menu.
- Floki: `https://floki.com/` has mascot-led/video-led hero content, ecosystem/project cards, buy pathways, updates, footer resources, and prominent calls to action.

## Adopted for Bye Buy
A simple one-page navigation, short hook, clear beginner steps, separate confirmed token facts, community CTA, FAQ, and risk note. Preserve Bye Buy’s own single-scroll page and narrative transition from sunny greeting to deep ocean. Do not copy their visual identities, copy, mascots, claims, or interactions.

## Limits
The browser-accessible text was reviewed; exact responsive breakpoints, motion behavior, and every visual detail were not verified. Some pages are dynamic. No claim of exhaustive internal-page inspection is made.
